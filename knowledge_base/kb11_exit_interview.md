# Exit Interview Process

An exit interview is scheduled once a resignation has been accepted, and it is normally held in the final week of the notice period. The conversation covers the reason for leaving, feedback on the manager and the team, and any unresolved issue the employee wants placed on record. Taking part is voluntary, and an employee who declines does not lose any part of the final settlement or the relieving document. Individual exit responses go to the human-resources team only, and managers receive aggregated themes once at least five exits have been recorded for their unit.
